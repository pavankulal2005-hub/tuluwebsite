About Tulu

A language of coastal Karnataka

Tulu is a Dravidian language spoken especially in the coastal region of Karnataka and parts of Kerala. It has a rich oral tradition, local dialects and expressive everyday vocabulary.

This website is designed as a simple Kannada-to-Tulu beginner companion. It groups useful vocabulary into practical topics and offers quick revision activities.

How to learn effectively

  1. Learn 5–10 words every day.
  2. Say each word aloud and use it in a sentence.
  3. Practise with family members or fluent speakers.
  4. Take the quiz again to revise older words.
Important: This is a starter learning resource, not a certified dictionary. Tulu varies by region; please verify uncommon or formal expressions with native speakers.
